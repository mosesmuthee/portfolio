A little about me

Curious by nature. Practical by design.

I like understanding how things work, finding where they can work better, and building the software to make that happen.

Currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics and Computer Science at Murang’a University of Technology.

I care about the problem behind the code.

My experience spans backend development, API integration, database design, Android development and deployment. I enjoy moving between the different parts of a product: understanding requirements, shaping the data and application structure, building the interface, connecting services and helping get the result into users' hands.

I've worked with teams on products designed for real communities and everyday needs. One example is AquaSense, a satellite-based water-monitoring solution that placed third at the Build Zero Hackathon. I've also contributed to projects including ChamaSpark and LinkedLine Connect.

Studying mathematics strengthens the way I approach engineering: break a complex problem down, look for its structure, test assumptions and work towards a solution that makes sense. I'm continuing to grow in full-stack engineering, cloud technologies, DevOps and applied AI.
